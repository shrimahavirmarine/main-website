Welcome aboard

From the Himalayas to the high seas

Himalayan Maritime Academy (HMA) is a premier international maritime training institution in Lalitpur, Nepal, approved by the Merchant Marine of Honduras.

We develop competent, confident and safety-conscious maritime professionals through internationally recognised education and practical training — combining academic excellence, experienced faculty and a student-focused approach.

Whether you are starting your maritime journey, renewing your Certificate of Competency, upgrading your qualifications or attending refresher training, HMA gives you the knowledge, skills and support to succeed.

  • Internationally approved maritime training
  • Faculty of Master Mariners, Chief Engineers and industry professionals
  • Maritime education that builds professionalism, leadership and discipline
