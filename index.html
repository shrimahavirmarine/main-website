Welcome aboard

Welcome to Himalayan Maritime Academy

Himalayan Maritime Academy (HMA) is a premier international maritime training institution located in Nepal and approved by the Honduras Maritime Administration.

Our Academy is dedicated to developing competent, confident and safety-conscious maritime professionals through internationally recognised education and practical training. We combine academic excellence, experienced faculty and a student-focused approach to prepare seafarers for rewarding careers in the global shipping industry.

Whether you are starting your maritime journey, renewing your Certificate of Competency, upgrading your professional qualifications or attending refresher training, HMA provides the knowledge, skills and support needed to succeed.

Maritime education is not only about obtaining certificates — it is about building professionalism, leadership, discipline and lifelong competence.

Frequently asked questions

Before you apply

Who can apply?
Aspiring seafarers, deck officers, engine officers, ratings and experienced maritime professionals.
Can I renew my COC?
Yes. Eligible candidates can enrol in our COC renewal programmes.
Can I upgrade my COC?
Yes. Professional upgradation programmes are available for eligible candidates, in both the deck and engine departments.
Is accommodation available?
Yes. Boarding, lodging and meals are included in the course fee — there are no separate charges.
Are classes conducted in English?
Yes. All training is conducted in English by experienced maritime professionals.
How do I apply?
Applications can be submitted through the form on this page, or by contacting our Admissions Office directly.
